Lumiq Cinema privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Lumiq Cinema ("the app") is a video player for Meta Quest headsets. This policy, from the developer of Lumiq Cinema, explains what the app does with information.

In short: we don't collect, sell or share your personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your headset

The app saves the following on your headset only, so it works the way you left it:

  • Your library settings, favourites, recently played list, resume positions and per-video format choices.
  • The network servers you add (address, share or folder, username and password). Passwords and server details are stored encrypted with the headset's Android Keystore.
  • If you use the Private section, a salted hash of your PIN (never the PIN itself) and the list of videos you've hidden.
  • Theatre preferences such as screen size, position and environment.

None of this is sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. You can also clear your history in the app (Recent → Clear history) and remove servers at any time.

Network access

The app only connects to:

  • Servers you choose. These are SMB file shares, DLNA/UPnP media servers and WebDAV servers that you add or pick, used to browse and stream your videos.
  • Your local network, when you search for shares. When you tap Find shares, and when you open the Network section (to find DLNA servers), the app sends standard discovery messages on your local network only (Bonjour/mDNS, WS-Discovery, NetBIOS name queries and connection checks on file-sharing ports). The results are shown to you and aren't stored unless you save a server.
  • Meta's platform, to confirm your purchase. When the app starts, it uses the Meta Horizon platform to check that the signed-in account owns the app. Meta handles this check under Meta's Privacy Policy. The app doesn't receive or keep any of your personal details from it beyond whether the check passed.

Your videos and their contents never leave your network through the app.

Permissions

  • Video files: to list and play videos stored on the headset or a connected USB drive.
  • Network and Wi-Fi multicast: to stream from your servers and find them on your local network.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
