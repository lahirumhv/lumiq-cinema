Lumiq Cinema

A private cinema for your own videos, for Meta Quest.

Watch your own movies, 3D and VR videos on a giant screen, from your headset, a USB drive or your home network (SMB, DLNA, WebDAV). There's no account, no subscription and no tracking.

  • Flat, 3D side-by-side and top-bottom, 180° and 360° video, detected automatically.
  • Find shares scans your home network for PCs, Macs and NAS drives, from SMB1 to SMB 3.1.1.
  • Drag the screen anywhere; hold B to bring it in front of you, even lying down.
  • Cinema, space, void and passthrough environments.
